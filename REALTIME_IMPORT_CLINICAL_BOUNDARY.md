# RealTime import clinical-boundary rule

Source-recorded pre-eclampsia status is never an adjudication answer.

Fields such as `RECORDED_PE_STATUS`, recorded diagnosis, diagnosis date, final diagnosis or similar site assertions are classified as **restricted comparison metadata**. They are excluded from adjudicator-facing canonical evidence and from every DV/longitudinal derivation input.

The system may calculate and display dated evidence criteria—hypertension, proteinuria, organ dysfunction, uteroplacental findings, completeness, certainty limitations and a neutral earliest qualifying-evidence date. This is advisory evidence support, not a PE determination.

Permitted wording: “Earliest visit where configured evidence criteria coexist.”

Prohibited wording: “The participant developed PE at this visit.”

Only an authorised independent adjudicator, or a properly constituted committee when required, can determine PE status, diagnosis, onset classification, severity and certainty. A recorded site diagnosis may be compared after restricted Monitor review solely to identify discrepancies or a possible query; it cannot populate the reviewer form.
